About This Project

Lausse Hub

The story behind this site, and some FAQs about Lausse and the upcoming album & era.

Our Mission

The Lausse Hub project was born out of a deep appreciation for the mysterious, evolving, and deeply personal music of Lausse The Cat. As he moves between aliases like Blu Man and Don Papa 海賊, his full discography can be fragmented and difficult to track.

Our mission is simple: to create the definitive, comprehensive, and chronological fan-made timeline of all his known tracks, features, and production credits. This includes everything from the early Soundcloud beats to his most streamed tracks like Redstripe Rhapsody.

We aim to be the community's central resource, where fans can easily listen, read lyrics, and share insights on every step of his journey.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who created the Lausse Hub website?

This website was lovingly created by a dedicated fan of Lausse The Cat. It is entirely non-commercial and built solely to celebrate his discography and musical journey.

Is this the official Lausse The Cat website?

No, this is a fan-made project. The official website is lausseshouse.com

Is there Lausse the Cat merch?

There appears to be plans based on the "Only at live shows" merchandise section of the official website. This is yet to be confirmed but seems a strong hint that live shows and merchandise are coming.

Can I contribute to the Lausse Hub project?

Absolutely! We welcome contributions, especially for rare track lyrics (email me at jacobrog7@gmail.com), corrected information, or new details. Please join the conversation on r/Laussethecat to submit your suggestions.

What is Lausse The Cat's most popular song?

His most popular track is currently Redstripe Rhapsody.
